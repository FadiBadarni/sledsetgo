Support

How to play

  • Pull the sling back and let go to throw the otter.
  • Drag left and right to steer the sled.
  • Swipe up in the air to flip, or on the snow to hop over a log or a rock.
  • Finish a realm to open the next; finish the Mountain to open its endless mode.

Your progress

Your progress, coins and looks are kept on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them, and they can't be recovered.
